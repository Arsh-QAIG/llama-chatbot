# Product overview
Q-Cluster is a scalable quantum computing simulator designed for educators, learners, and researchers. Scale upto 24 qubits with hybrid CPU-GPU infrastructure.

# Product features:
CPU and GPU (T4/V100/A100/H100) support — Simulate up to 24 qubits ideal, 9 qubits with noise.

Noise-Aware Learning
Gate errors, decoherence, readout noise — Simulate realistic quantum hardware behavior with configurable noise models.

Algorithm Development
Build & test quantum applications — Prototyping support for Grover's, QFT, VQE, QAOA, QNNs and more.

Quantum Error Correction
QEC codes & logical gates — Learn 3, 5, 7-qubit codes with encoding/decoding and fidelity analysis.

Performance Metrics
Detailed simulation insights — Analyze noise impact, execution time, and simulation fidelity data.

Educational Mode
Learn quantum fundamentals — Guided tutorials, circuit design tasks, and QPU behavior approximation modules.

Learning Outcomes
Master quantum computing from fundamentals to advanced applications


Quantum Algorithm Learning
Understand and implement foundational quantum algorithms on scalable systems.


Noise Simulation
Develop intuition on how errors affect quantum computations with configurable noise.

Quantum Error Correction

QPU Performance Approximation

Application Prototyping
